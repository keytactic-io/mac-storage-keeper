First release of Storage Keeper: a menu bar app and CLI that track where disk space goes on your Mac, who's responsible, and what you can safely clean up.

## Highlights

- **Disk sweep and growth tracking:** snapshots of the whole Data volume, hidden folders included. The app shows which apps and folders grew over 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days.
- **Who's responsible:** space is attributed to apps and tools such as Playwright, npm, Xcode, Docker, Hugging Face, browsers, and project `node_modules`. Downloads show the app that downloaded them.
- **Live monitor:** a menu bar badge shows a `+` while a folder grows, orange or red for large bursts, and a red `!` when space is low. Notifications link to the folder in Finder.
- **Clean up:** suggestions for developer and AI caches, unused `node_modules`, old downloads, large files, and leftovers from removed apps. Every action asks for confirmation, and files go to the Trash, where Put Back works.
- **Safety dots:** an orange dot marks app data and a red dot marks macOS-managed folders. Moving macOS-managed folders to the Trash is blocked.
- **CLI:** `skeeper scan | top | growth | recommend | clean | watch | safety`.

## Install

1. Download **StorageKeeper-1.0.0.dmg**, open it, and drag Storage Keeper to Applications.
2. The app isn't notarized by Apple. On first launch, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**, or run:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Storage Keeper.app"`
3. For complete scans, grant **Full Disk Access** in Privacy & Security, then click **Relaunch** in the app.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Universal (Apple Silicon and Intel). Verify downloads with `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.
